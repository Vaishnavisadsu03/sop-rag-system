SOP TITLE: Lab Equipment Usage
DEPARTMENT: Research_Department
VERSION: v1.0

1.0 Purpose
This SOP defines the standard procedure for lab equipment usage in the Research_Department.

2.0 Safety and Compliance
All personnel must follow organizational safety guidelines.
Unauthorized access is prohibited.

3.0 Pre-Operation Checks
Verify readiness of systems or equipment.
Ensure authorization before proceeding.

4.0 Operating Procedure
Execute steps defined for lab equipment usage.
Monitor activities continuously.

5.0 Shutdown or Closure Procedure
Safely stop activities.
Secure systems and update logs.
